The data was there.
The reporting layer wasn't.

Commission Reconciliation · US Aviation Company · 2023–2026

The ERP held open commissions. QuickBooks held some payments — but not all of them, because bank transactions weren't always linked in time. Three sources, none connected, no one reconciling across them. Sales reps were owed money. Nobody knew how much, or for how long.

Working with my team, we connected all three — ERP, QuickBooks, and the bank — and ran the first full reconciliation across three years of activity. What surfaced was $70,682.71 in unverified commissions. More importantly, people got paid. The report format and reporting standard you see below is mine — built to give leadership and the team a clear, complete, decision-ready picture. This covers one agent, the most significant, but the same process was applied across the full team.
